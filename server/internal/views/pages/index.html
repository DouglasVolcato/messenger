Messenger

A small messaging product designed to explore distributed-system architecture.

Create companies, connect users, subscribe to chats, exchange direct or group messages, react, and receive notifications.

The business domain is deliberately small so the project can focus on concurrency, realtime delivery, idempotency, queues, retries, caching, fanout and failure handling.
